# R-29 · Exercise overview

Priority: P1 · Spec: 08.6 · Where: Simulator

Precondition: a strength, a power and an interval exercise each logged at least once.

## Steps

1. Library → open a strength exercise.
2. Open a power and an interval exercise.
3. Tap the watch-how-to-perform action.

## Expect

- Step 1: tiles with the exercise's last and best results.
- Step 2: tiles fit the type; power and interval also show their settings, read-only.
- Step 3: a web search for the exercise opens outside the app.
