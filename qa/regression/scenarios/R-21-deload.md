# R-21 · Deload week

Priority: P0 · Spec: 03, 08.11 · Where: Simulator

Precondition: cycle A active, Weeks 1–3 done, Week 4 Day 1 not started. To get there, log and
finish every workout of Weeks 2 and 3 with any values.

## Steps

1. From the ⊞ grid open Week 3 Day 1 and note the weight and reps logged in each set of "Lateral
   Raise".
2. Open Week 4 Day 1.
3. Close the dialog.
4. Look at the first strength exercise, "Lateral Raise".
5. Open the header ⋯, then an exercise's ⋯.
6. Log and finish the workout.

## Expect

- Step 2: a one-time dialog introduces the deload week (lighter load, less volume, go easy).
- Step 4:
  - a deload badge instead of an RIR number, on every card and in the header;
  - fewer sets than in Week 3;
  - half the Week 3 weight prefilled;
  - each set's reps placeholder equals the reps noted in step 1 for that set;
  - no ⓘ by the weight column; after logging only a ✓, no hit indicator.
- Step 5: the header ⋯ has no "Add exercise"; the exercise ⋯ still has "Replace exercise". Close
  menus with care: "Stop cycle" is in the header menu.
- Step 6: the summary does not promise progression for a next week.
