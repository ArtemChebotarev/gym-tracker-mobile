## Frequently asked questions

**Where is my training data stored?**
Only on your phone, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud copy. See the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**I deleted the app. Can I get my workouts back?**
No. Deleting the app deletes its data, and there is no backup or sync yet. Please keep the app
installed while you want to keep your history.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Everything works without an internet connection.

**Is Hyperbro free?**
Yes, during early access.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Hyperbro is a training log; its suggestions are calculated from what you log. Consult a doctor
before starting a new exercise programme, and stop if you feel pain or dizziness. Read the full
[health notice](privacy-policy.md#7-health-notice).
